# 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Sea Adventure

A simple 3D pirate sailing game you can play in your browser.

## How to play (easiest way)

1. Go to this page:  
   **https://github.com/nicomodanlou0-ui/pirate-sea-adventure**

2. Click the green **Code** button

3. Click **Download ZIP**

4. Unzip the folder on your computer

5. Open the folder and **double-click `index.html`**

The game should open in your browser.

### Controls
- **W** or **↑** = Sail forward
- **S** or **↓** = Sail backward
- **A** or **←** = Turn left
- **D** or **→** = Turn right

That's it. Have fun sailing!
